Uses the first part of Corollary 1.4 (`T0020`). Let $d = \dim_k \mathfrak{m}/\mathfrak{m}^2$.
This space is finite-dimensional because $\mathfrak{m}$ is finitely generated.
Take a basis $e_1, \dots, e_d$ of $\mathfrak{m}/\mathfrak{m}^2$ and lift each $e_i$ to some
$x_i \in \mathfrak{m}$. Their residues span $\mathfrak{m}/\mathfrak{m}^2$, so by `T0020` the $x_i$
generate $\mathfrak{m}$. Conversely, if $a_1, \dots, a_n$ generate $\mathfrak{m}$, then each
$a_i \in \mathfrak{m}$. By `T0020` their $n$ residues span $\mathfrak{m}/\mathfrak{m}^2$, and a
space spanned by $n$ vectors has dimension at most $n$ (`finrank_range_le_card`).
