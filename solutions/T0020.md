This follows the book's proof and uses Nakayama's lemma, Proposition 1.3 (`T0019`), as a
dependency. Regard $\mathfrak{m}$ as an $A$-module. It is finitely generated because $A$ is
Noetherian. Let $N \subseteq \mathfrak{m}$ be the submodule generated by $a_1, \dots, a_n$.
The kernel of $\mathfrak{m} \to \mathfrak{m}/\mathfrak{m}^2$ is $\mathfrak{m}\cdot\mathfrak{m}$.
So $N$ maps onto $\mathfrak{m}/\mathfrak{m}^2$ iff $\mathfrak{m} = N + \mathfrak{m}\mathfrak{m}$,
iff $N = \mathfrak{m}$ by Nakayama (b). The action of $A$ on $\mathfrak{m}/\mathfrak{m}^2$ factors
through $k = A/\mathfrak{m}$. Hence the $k$-span of the residues is the image of the $A$-span,
i.e. the image of $N$. Finally, generating $\mathfrak{m}$ as a submodule of itself is the same as
generating it as an ideal of $A$.
