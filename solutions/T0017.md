Pairwise coprimality $\mathfrak{a}_i + \mathfrak{a}_j = A$ is Mathlib's `IsCoprime` for ideals.
For pairwise coprime ideals the product equals the intersection, which we get by induction on the
index set (`Ideal.prod_eq_iInf_of_pairwise_isCoprime`). The kernel of
$A \to \prod A/\mathfrak{a}_i$ is $\bigcap \mathfrak{a}_i$ for any family (`Ideal.ker_Pi_Quotient_mk`).
Surjectivity is Mathlib's Chinese Remainder Theorem (`Ideal.pi_quotient_surjective`), which builds,
as in the book, an element congruent to $1$ modulo $\mathfrak{a}_i$ and to $0$ modulo the other
ideals.
