We show that each condition is equivalent to `IsNoetherian R R`, i.e. that $A$ is a Noetherian
$A$-module. (a) is the definition (`isNoetherian_def`). (b) is `monotone_stabilizes_iff_noetherian`
after converting between a monotone sequence and an order homomorphism `ℕ →o Ideal R`. (c) is
`set_has_maximal_iff_noetherian`, which phrases maximality as "no member of $\Sigma$ properly
contains $\mathfrak{c}$". That is equivalent to "every member of $\Sigma$ containing $\mathfrak{c}$
equals $\mathfrak{c}$". Then `tfae_finish` assembles the equivalences.
